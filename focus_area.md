## Behavioral
Prepare 8–10 strong stories from Amazon/Adobe. Use STAR, but don't make them sound scripted.

### Stories to prepare
1. Biggest technical challenge
2. Most impactful project
3. Major production incident
4. Difficult technical decision
5. Disagreement with another engineer
6. Disagreement with manager
7. Failure / mistake
8. Project that didn't go according to plan
9. Situation where you influenced without authority
10. Mentoring another engineer
11. Ambiguous problem you drove
12. Tight deadline
13. Handling competing priorities
14. Architecture decision you owned
15. Example of improving engineering efficiency

### And for every story, know:
```
What was the problem?
Why was it difficult?
What did YOU do?
What alternatives existed?
Why did you choose your approach?
What was the result?
What would you do differently?
```
